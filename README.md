# Shade Safety : ម្លប់សុវត្ថិភាព
> **"Recognise the Signs. Protect Your Health."**
> **«ទទួលស្គាល់សញ្ញា ការពារសុខភាពរបស់អ្នក»**

## 🎯 What the App Does
1. **Target Priority Sectors**:
   - 🧵 **Garment Workers** (*កម្មករកាត់ដេរ*): Ventilation guidance for tin-roofed factories (indoor heat reaching 31–41°C).
   - 🛺 **Tuk-Tuk & PassApp Drivers** (*អ្នករត់កង់បី*): Protection against direct solar radiation, asphalt heat, and hydration planning.
   - 🍳 **Street Food Sellers** (*អ្នកលក់ដូរតាមផ្លូវ*): Heat defense from cooking burner stoves, sidewalk radiation, and shaded setups.

2. **Core Features**:
   - 🗺️ **Zoom Earth Heat Stress Radar Map**: Interactive wet-bulb and temperature radar for 12 Cambodian provinces, timeline controls, and NOAA Heat Index calculations.
   - 🩺 **6 Heat Illness Symptom Recognition**: Actionable guides for Heat Rash, Heat Cramps, Heat Syncope, Heat Exhaustion, and life-threatening Heat Stroke.
   - 🚰 **Hourly Rest & Hydration Checklist**: Interactive water tracker and work-rest cycle timer (1 cup every 20-30 min, 5-10 min shade rest).
   - 🚨 **Emergency Hotlines**: Direct one-tap calling to **119** (24/7 Free Ambulance) and **115** (Ministry of Health).
